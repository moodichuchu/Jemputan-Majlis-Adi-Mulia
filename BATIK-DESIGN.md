# Traditional batik design

The traditional textile asset is `img/traditional-batik.png`, created with the built-in imagegen tool. It replaces the earlier geometric SVG backdrop and is used for the envelope flap and the invitation's top border. The body of the invitation remains warm, plain paper for readable text.

Generation prompt:

Create a seamless repeating traditional Javanese batik textile pattern tile, square, flat top-down cloth scan. Authentic-looking hand drawn wax-resist parang-inspired diagonal flowing S-shaped knife and wave motifs, alternating narrow diagonal bands of intricate tiny cream dots (isen), delicate floral tendrils and leaf infill. Dense detailed artisan textile, irregular fine wax lines and subtle natural dye texture, elegant classic Central Javanese sogan palette: rich dark chocolate brown, warm tan, aged ivory, restrained very dark indigo. No modern geometric logos, no large empty shapes, no gold metallic effects, no text, no people, no borders, no mockup, no folds, no lighting gradient. Pattern must extend to all four edges and repeat seamlessly. Museum-quality traditional batik cloth appearance, fine intricate detail with clear readable diagonal rhythm.

Visual research: [Smithsonian Javanese parang batik](https://www.si.edu/object/javanese-kain-panjang-or-hip-wrapper-parang-pattern-batik-1915%3Anmah_627091). The generated design is an interpretation, not a reproduction of that textile.
